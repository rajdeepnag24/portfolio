MBA candidate · Former software engineer · Product thinker

I’m interested in
how things make sense.

I’m Rajdeep — currently pursuing my MBA at ISB, after studying Computer Engineering at UC San Diego and working as a software engineer. I’ve moved from building systems to asking what systems should exist, who they should serve, and how to make them genuinely useful.

My throughline is curiosity about how people, ideas and technology fit together. I like getting close to a problem, finding the useful signal in the mess, and turning it into an experience people can understand and use.

That instinct shows up in product discovery, interface design, code — and in the way I paint.
