About Me

My name is Rajdeep Nag,
but everybody calls me Raj.

I'm an artist and designer with a background in software engineering.

I've been painting in acrylic for a few years, drawn to scenes that mix everyday life with a bit of atmosphere - city streets, quiet interiors, and landscapes.

Alongside painting, I studied Computer Engineering at UCSD and worked as a software developer, which led me into a handful of design and development projects as well. I move comfortably between painting, design, and development, and I'm always looking to learn and take on new creative challenges.

Outside of this, I love watching movies, making videos, painting, playing sports like tennis and football, the World Cup kind, and I've also recently become pretty good at cooking.
